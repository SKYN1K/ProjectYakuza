# Lau Ka Long spear-ground emote

Hold any usable spear-class weapon, stand still without aiming, and choose Emote wheel → Project Yakuza Emotes → Lau Ka Long: Spear Ground Strike. The standalone addon does not require Project Yakuza combat or Custom Voice Framework. Majima's bat gesture and Kiryu's empty-hand gesture remain in the same category.

The original Kiwami main12_s04 CMN16 tree binds Lau and his independent spear to cut 007 actor/prop GMTs and the matching cut 008 continuation. Playback uses frames 112..190 from cut 007 and frames 1..225 from cut 008. Cut 007's last pose and cut 008's first pose match exactly; the duplicate boundary is included once. The result has 304 original samples at 30 fps over 10.1 seconds, adding exactly five seconds to version 0.2.3. Original cut-007 frame 121 supplies the floor strike. The added motion continues the flourish rather than stretching playback or holding the last frame.

All 22 native spear items are supported, including short/long forged, crafted, fire-hardened, crude, stone, glass, bone, plunger and knife/fork/scissor/screwdriver variants. Indexed native model sprites are included. The addon selects a native model-specific clip from the single spear menu entry; every variant preserves the same source timing and body motion. Held items are retained without substitution.

Each native mesh receives shaft-grip and floor-contact calibration. Native arm lengths are preserved. During the continuation's low sweep, a bounded two-bone right-arm clearance keeps the hand and weapon above the support plane while preserving original wrist/weapon rotation. The largest clearance is below 0.088 native units. Soles remain grounded. Every original floor strike reaches the native support plane within 0.00002 native units, and all 6688 native model/frame combinations stay above that plane within the same tolerance.

Unknown modded weapons with SubCategory Spear or SwingAnim Spear are accepted using the crafted-spear fallback. Their custom geometry is not individually calibrated, so unusual lengths, origins or attachments may have different floor alignment. Broken weapons, unrelated off-hand items, hidden weapons, unsafe native states and active/queued actions remain blocked.

Playback uses native cancellable emote state, end events and replicated parameters. It adds no damage, Heat, protection, items, textures, models, voice or sounds. Both multiplayer peers need matching addon files. See lau-spear-emote-assets.json for source/segment hashes, 22 native profiles, exact sample mapping, per-frame grip/clearance data and provenance. Offline tests pass; Project Zomboid was not launched for visual or live multiplayer testing.

The character follows the original center-inclusive movement through native deferred Translation_Data. Both cut segments retain their own source motion with a constant stage-origin alignment at their matching boundary. All 22 spear-specific clips use translation-only native movement, with no deferred rotation or Lua position correction.
