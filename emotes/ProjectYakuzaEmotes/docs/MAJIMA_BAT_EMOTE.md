# Majima bat-point emote

Hold a usable baseball bat, stop moving and aiming, then open the normal emote wheel and choose Project Yakuza Emotes → Majima: Bat Point (hold a bat). Supported native items are BaseballBat, BaseballBat_Crafted, BaseballBat_Metal, BaseballBat_Nails, ShortBat and ShortBat_Nails in the Base module. The secondary hand may be empty or hold the same two-handed item. An unrelated off-hand item, broken or hidden bat, active combat action or pending timed action prevents playback.

The gesture is independent of equipped combat styles and has no Heat gain, cost, damage, threat, protection or voice playback. It uses the existing native held item and model. PZ controls emote completion and cancellation; this feature adds no polling, custom network packets or combat action lease.

The integration follows the native-emote approach of [Custom Voice Framework](https://github.com/DmitryFisk/custom-voice-framework/tree/e0f46546cbbddb864671d9a14887987b3900e2a5). The original framework and separate voice pack are unchanged. Project Yakuza adds its own category after the native menu rebuild and delegates all other selections to the previous handler. It works with either CVF loading order and also without CVF. From CVF's Yakuza 0 submenu, use Back to reach the native category list.

The supplied Kiwami archive is media/data/auth_w64_e/main08_s02.par. Its CMN16 tree binds c_at_majima_U1_S185 to the actor motion and a08_02a_bat_metal_5 to the independently animated bat. Cut 002 resolves these resources to main08_002_6.gmt and main08_002_18.gmt. The original subtitles identify the opening “It’s been a while... Kiryu-chan!” greeting. The emote retains frames 70..160 inclusive at 30 fps: 91 samples over 3 seconds, beginning just after the source’s internal shot cut and covering the bat behind his head, forward point and lowering. The earlier incorrect version used a later cut 009 gesture. A constant yaw makes the point face forward. PZ limb lengths, supplied native bat fingers, a calibrated palm/grip and per-frame root-Y sole correction adapt the original data without inventing motion samples.

The selector plays ProjectYakuza_MajimaBatPoint in the native emote animation group with full-body and prop weights, a 0.25-second blend and one EmoteFinishing event. Native PlayerEmoteState carries the emote name and playing flag for multiplayer; no combat observer clip is substituted. Every peer needs the same animation and selector. Offline checks verify native state parameters and asset selection; an actual multiplayer session was not run.

See majima-bat-emote-assets.json for source hashes, exact role/resource bindings, frame selection, source yaw, original bat orientations, native grip and grounding corrections. See VALIDATION_0.2.2.md for the corrected selection and current validation; VALIDATION_0.104.0.md records the earlier integration. No textures, icons, models or audio were generated or replaced.

This gesture is supplied by the standalone Project Yakuza Emotes mod from version 0.1.0. Project Yakuza combat is optional.
