# Bat Heat Action

Dragon of Dojima now has the original Yakuza 6 held long-blunt Heat Action from h23710_buki_d.par. Equip a supported bat, hold physical RMB, and press the Heat Action binding (MMB by default) near an eligible standing target while outside a combo. It costs one Heat stock, or the equivalent active Extreme Heat fuel. Payment occurs once when native playback is confirmed; a missing animation costs nothing.

Supported items are Base.BaseballBat, Base.BaseballBat_Crafted, Base.BaseballBat_Metal, Base.BaseballBat_Nails, Base.ShortBat and Base.ShortBat_Nails. Other items sharing the ordinary bat/blunt profile, including pipes, bars and nightsticks, retain their ordinary moves and cannot borrow this scene. Other styles do not receive it. Normal weapon-heavy input during a combo is unchanged.

The supplied CMN18 scene binds m_kiryu to h23710_000_4 and the final enemy character-motion layer to h23710_000_7. Its earlier h23710_000_6 is another enemy body binding, not a separate bat. The weapon attaches to the actor's animated buki1_r_n bone. All 103 original samples are retained at 30 fps, giving 3.4 seconds of playback. The original vector translation, elevation and rotation are retained in the native paired paths and root. Arm directions are fitted to PZ's segment lengths. The bat's full original weapon-bone orientation and hand-relative movement are converted; its grip is not fixed for the whole scene.

The source long-blunt models bs_d000_type_d_bou and bs_d001_type_d_bat, plus PZ's native Baseballbat.x, all extend along local positive Y. Numeric checks verify this coordinate correspondence. The installed native bat model is used directly; no model, texture, image or synthetic animation is created. The source model references are extracted only for validation and are excluded from the mod and release.

The two original victim damage nodes start at frames 14 and 50, weighted 500/1500. The new action deals 1.5 times the held bat's canonical average base damage, split into 0.375 and 1.125. The source damage markers are independent of the earlier physical motion, sound and particle starts. Native Hit does not restart the victim track. Lethal damage is held until full recovery; interruption discards reserved lethal damage. Multiplayer computes damage from the server's held weapon and ignores client damage amounts and duplicate contacts.

Five original non-voice hact_h23710_buki_d bank streams use their exact CMN starts: first bat hit at frame 1, floor effect at 15, body fall at 25, swing at 31 and second bat hit at 39. Actor voice roles at frames 4 and 24 use existing CombatHeavyAttack and CombatSpecialMove provider entries. Six original blood-family emitter starts use sampled native victim attachments and existing supplied blood pixels. Non-blood engine particles and cinematic cameras are not recreated. The separate voice pack is unchanged.

bat-heat-assets.json records source archive/scene/GMT hashes, resource GUID assignments, weapon-bone conversion, native mesh provenance and paired paths. heat-scenes.json records original damage, audio and particle events. Native owner, zombie/PvP victim and both MP observer cycles have isolated selectors and no collision events. Full offline evidence is in [validation](VALIDATION_0.96.0.md). Restart with matching updated client/server copies; live presentation remains to be checked in gameplay.
