# Open-space grab Heat Action

Equip Dragon of Dojima and grab a standing zombie with RMB plus the configured Grab binding, G by default. During the held phase, keep RMB held and press the Heat Action binding, MMB by default. One full Heat stock is sufficient. The original wall action takes priority beside a suitable solid wall. Otherwise, a shoulder throw and follow-up is available when both complete paired paths fit in open space. The same held zombie remains the target; nearby zombies do not change the selection.

The supplied Yakuza 6 source is h23600_seoi_oi.par. Its original CMN character/motion GUIDs bind the player to h23600_000_2.gmt and the single victim to h23600_000_4.gmt. No wall, prop, weapon or other opponent is needed. Both clips retain all 225 original samples at 30 fps, lasting 224/30 seconds. CMN victim damage starts at frames 117 and 167. The mod retains its existing total five-times canonical unarmed multiplier, distributed using the original 1500:250 weights; source force-death flags are not applied. Lethal damage settles after the complete sequence.

Original arm directions are fitted to native Human segment lengths. Native actor soles supply the support plane, and bounded victim surface clearance prevents the shorter native body from penetrating the floor during the landing/follow-up. Original body rotations, finger template, sample timing and numeric paired paths remain intact. Six scoped native selectors cover owner playback, both multiplayer observer cycles and the zombie idle/hit-reaction states, with full-body descendant masks and no native collision events.

All nine original non-voice hact_h23600.acb streams are decoded unchanged from the supplied sound.par. Original cue IDs, cue-sheet binding and independent sound/voice starts are retained. Voice labels use the existing combat voice integration; the separate voice pack is untouched. Two native victim head anchors drive existing supplied hit/blood presentation at the damage frames. No textures, models, camera controls or custom Java bridge are added.

The client and authoritative server resolve the current style's grabHeat variants. Both validate held-target ownership, geometry and available Heat; the server independently preserves wall priority and rejects unknown/mismatched variants. A matched request token keeps the local grab alive across the server release/grant handoff. New obstructions before the handoff retain the old grab and Heat. Failed or interrupted starts before native playback confirmation do not charge the stock. Both complete paths are checked again before startup. Protection and cleanup use the shared paired-action lifecycle, and multiplayer actors remain visible.

Both peers need matching combat files. Offline checks pass, but Project Zomboid was not launched for visual blending or live multiplayer testing. Source archive, rig, scene, clip, audio and native presentation provenance are recorded in open-grab-heat-assets.json, open-grab-heat-scene.json and open-grab-heat-presentation.json.
