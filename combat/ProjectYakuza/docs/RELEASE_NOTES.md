# Project Yakuza 0.135.0

- Heat Action trails now select the striking limb per contact rather than forcing all weapon scenes onto the right arm. The knife uses its right arm for the stab and right leg for the kick. The katana uses its right arm, left punching arm, then right arm. The beer crate uses the weapon arm, alternating right/left/right/left kicks, then the weapon arm for its ending. Bottle, bat and bicycle weapon strikes retain their weapon-arm tracks; wall, stomp, grab and Extreme Heat scenes retain their original individual limbs and ending contact IDs.
- Rebuilt source-sampled limb tracks and matching victim anchors from the existing supplied clips. No animation, model, texture, sound or music bytes were changed. Local and observer paired scenes share the same contact catalogue, confirmation masks and native animation clock; switching limbs clears the previous coating history.
- Corrected a CMN scene-data override that retained the old Extreme Heat contact clock after the lead-in was shortened. Damage, blood emitters, audio/voice events, limb windows and victim anchors now use the same source-frame map. The original animation speed, QTE windows and failure sequence are retained.
- Prepared the runtime-only Build 42 install and Workshop packages. Framework documentation/instructions, AGENTS.md, extraction/build tools, provenance reports, previews, backups, repository metadata and private paths/credentials remain outside the upload. The separate emotes, voice pack and optional phone dev add-on are excluded. The complete publishing guide is kept in docs/WORKSHOP.md in GitHub.

Validation covers all 95 existing attack clips and 121 contact windows, explicit mixed-limb owner/observer sequences in eight facings, native limb projection, source hashes, remapped CMN timelines and original PCM checks, Kahlua Heat Action/presentation and multiplayer checks, comment removal, runtime references, package privacy and native B42 Workshop validation.
