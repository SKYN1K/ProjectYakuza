# Project Yakuza 0.134.0

- Limb, charging and impact sparks now mix three unchanged supplied Yakuza/Gaiden textures. Their geometry rotates around each original spark-head pivot, with random initial angles and both spin directions. Limb/charge sparks launch from the tracked limb, then fly independently through world space with varied velocity, curvature, drag, gravity and 100–270 ms lifetimes. Impact sparks have independent texture/spin and more varied directions. Texture dimensions and pivots keep the particles compact; black-backed textures use additive blending and transparent Gaiden glints use normal alpha. Smoke placement, white tint, existing drop and particle budgets are retained.
- Wall Heat Action victims now retain their paired clip in idle, hit-reaction, stagger, knocked-down stagger and getting-up animation states. During paired ownership, bump/fall/stagger flags and residual movement are cleared, and stagger immunity is leased and restored on cleanup. Zombie updates refresh the hold so collision reactions cannot replace the wall scene between player updates. The original wall animation is unchanged.
- Shortened the Extreme Heat lead-in before the first QTE by exactly 15 source frames / 500 ms. The first pose is retained and the opening joins through the existing three-frame source-pose blend. Both actor and victim clips now last 8.5 seconds. The six original nineteen-frame QTE sections, retained 30-fps pose spacing, 1x playback and failure ending are unchanged. QTE deadlines, native paired paths, damage/audio anchors and limb FK were rebuilt together.
- Added brief local impact treatment on confirmed Heat Action contacts: bounded camera shake, a restrained colour/tone pulse and red/cyan fringing at the actual victim contact point. This is a Lua approximation of Yakuza's screen treatment. Full-frame RGB scene separation and shader colour correction are not exposed to ordinary Lua, so no framebuffer reads, Java helper, shader replacement or persistent graphics/climate changes are installed. Shake accounts for aim-pan and zoom; visible camera offsets and blending restore after rendering, including pause, map, death, errors and cleanup. Split-screen views are excluded from this local camera treatment.

Offline validation includes source-frame/pose provenance, native imports at 8.5 seconds, rebuilt contact/path/FK data, native victim selection in all six states, real Lua-to-native rotated quad drawing and API checks, spark variety/spin/flight/pivots, paired bump suppression and release, QTE/presentation timing and camera/render ownership cleanup. Original texture bytes and pixels are retained. All 95 production Lua modules compile. Source-comment, runtime-reference and package privacy checks precede publication; installed bytes are verified. PZ was not launched, so final screen appearance, occlusion and frame pacing remain for an in-game check.

The existing phone dev 0.1.6 add-on uses this shared combat update without a separate phone installation change. Standalone emotes and the voice pack are unchanged.
