# Project Yakuza 0.112.0

- Bicycle carrying now uses the original Kiwami 2 heavy-object walking arms during ordinary walking. Six normal, slow and injured walk composites preserve native PZ leg motion, body motion, movement distances and footstep events. The bicycle-only selector competes with all native movement selectors and retains aimed, sneaking, tree and turning fallbacks.
- Replaced the bicycle model with Yakuza 6 bicycle B using its nine original lowest-detail parts, 570 source vertices and 496 triangles. Its original 512x512 diffuse is reduced to 128x128 using original nearest-neighbour texels. Recalibrated the hand grips across all attacks, holds, walking and the confirmed jump-crush. Rebased its dropped-world attachment and updated its display name.
- Automatic empty-hand restoration after removing a supported weapon no longer plays the style-switch cue. Actual style selection and reselection retain their cue and lifecycle cleanup remains active. selectStyle's optional feedback argument controls selection audio.
- Added Yakuza 6 console bank button_appear, button_suc and button_miss sounds for QTE prompts, accepted presses and failures. Playback is local 2D UI audio, follows the Sounds volume control and avoids duplicate timeout/failure cues. Addon QTE feedback overrides remain supported.
- Extreme Heat QTE victim and failure animations now follow the paired scene phase, including native state restarts, paused playback and multiplayer observer clocks. Original motion and damage timing remain unchanged. Reduced all eight Extreme Heat setup lights from 0.525 to 0.10 damage multiplier, about 81 percent less, while retaining ordinary attacks and the QTE payoff.
- Offline checks cover original archives, all 13 Kiwami 2 attack/hold/walk clips, original native movement selectors and six speed/injury blends, native grip/import/pixel contracts, 18 paired main/failure selectors and actual normalized track replay, all 31 Kahlua suites, 87 production Lua files, 452 MP selectors, runtime references and zero comments. Source, ZIP and installed bytes are verified. Project Zomboid was not launched; live playback and multiplayer still need an in-game check.
