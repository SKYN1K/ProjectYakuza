# Project Yakuza 0.122.0

- Reworked the limb coating into three translucent layers: a smaller, clearer flame core, middle smoke and a soft outer smoke layer about 20% wider than the previous flame coat. All 64 original Yakuza 6 smoke frames are reused unchanged. Outer particles remain faint, with a stronger inner layer; existing effect colours are preserved.
- Each attack gets fresh cosmetic phases. Individual particles vary their animation speed, size, opacity and tightly bounded swirl. Flying sparks now have varied curved paths, and small impact puffs alternate between three supplied Kiwami 2 shapes. Impact puffs, sparks and the large heavy-hit ring are smaller and briefer.
- Reduced apparent separation from limbs: smoke history is limited to 70 ms and tethered within two base-scale screen pixels of the current limb before a small swirl is applied. Recovery smoke follows the current source pose instead of remaining at an old world position. Fading duplicate clips are rechecked for the incoming clock. Coating fades out below strong attack ownership and is suppressed under 70% native track weight or when the native track disappears, avoiding full-pose effects during heavy blending. This remains baked source tracking rather than a read of the final blended skeleton.
- Offline validation passes 19,333 limb/clock/rotation/lifecycle/budget assertions, including 24 rapid-turn/movement samples, layered opacity and size bounds, fresh swing phases, overlapping copies, low-weight/missing-track suppression, recovery cleanup and smaller curved sparks. Paired presentation, blood, charge and multiplayer suites pass; all 89 production Lua files compile. Every pixel of all 64 smoke frames matches the original DDS atlas; native ImageData verifies all channels of 296,960 pixels across 68 original textures. All existing texture, model, animation, audio and selector bytes are unchanged. Source comments and runtime references are checked; packages and local installation are byte-verified. No new visual/audio assets, preview, game launch or Steam upload.
