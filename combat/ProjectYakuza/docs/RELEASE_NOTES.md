# Project Yakuza 0.133.0

- Reworked charged wind-up/hold effects around the existing animation-specific limb tracks: layered white smoke, compact original Gaiden wisps, varied motion and small flying sparks replace the old additive cores and flames. Eighteen particles per charged limb, at most eight actors; source animation speed is unchanged. Release swings retain the modern attack coating and contact effects.
- Ordinary and Heat Action limb coating is ten percent larger, with two-frequency motion and a smoke tip extending at most three centimetres past the hand/foot. Impact anchors remain on the actual tracked limb. White colours, restrained opacity, compact wisps, the 65 ms shrinking tail and existing particle budgets remain intact.
- Reduced first-use work: textures load six per tick, followed by one sprite/audio setup job per tick. Contact sprites, blood frames and the combat-music emitter warm before use. Staggering a smoke animation now uses one native update per particle rather than advancing through up to 63 updates. Unready textures still cannot render placeholders. Sound-file decoding, live driver behaviour and full gameplay timing require an in-game check.
- Space while running/sprinting outside aim cannot queue a fixed-direction quickstep or leave a delayed aim retry. Aiming and walking quicksteps retain their existing motions and quota. Running cannot hold a quickstep action open while waiting for a missing animation.
- Reverse double finishers now select their original custom nodes both with and without a recovery link. Previously the linked flag excluded every reverse follow-up node, allowing native fallback attacks.
- Custom unarmed running is selected while rested. Exertion or tiredness hands running back to the existing native movement blend until recovery. Observers use replicated native movement speed, polled at most four times per second, to select the slow blend. Player speed, fatigue penalties, source clips and attack cadence are unchanged.
- Low health pulses the health fill and its existing red frame. Removed CRITICAL and LOW HEALTH words. The bar remains visible through the pulse; existing private heartbeat behaviour is retained.

Offline checks cover actual native linked/unlinked reverse node selection, native exhausted/rested run selection, run/Space rejection, fatigue recovery and observer gates, charging cleanup/pause/actor budgets, enlarged coating and unchanged impact anchors, bounded warm-up and error recovery, and low-health flashing. Native animation import/world projection and original effect pixel/hash checks precede publication. 32,312 combat Kahlua assertions pass, with 18 native selector contracts and 23,232 native limb projection cases. All 93 production Lua modules compile. No game was launched; live appearance, frame pacing and multiplayer rendering remain for an in-game check.

The same combat source is used by the separate phone development build. Runtime ZIPs exclude tools, source-extraction folders, previews, credentials and standalone emotes.
