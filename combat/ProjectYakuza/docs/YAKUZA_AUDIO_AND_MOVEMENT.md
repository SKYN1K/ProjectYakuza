# Original Yakuza audio and movement fixes — 0.20.0

Original audio is extracted from the supplied Yakuza 6 `act_kiryu.acb`, `battle_se_kiryu.acb` and `battle_common.acb` banks. The CRI cue tables are resolved through sequences, tracks and synth references to the embedded AWB/HCA waveforms. Forty-five original waveforms are decoded and converted to mono 48 kHz OGG for PZ spatial playback; no replacement recordings or synthesized sounds are used. Source-bank hashes, cue names, waveform indices, AWB IDs and output hashes are recorded in `yakuza-combat-sounds.json`.

Sixteen native sound groups cover punch/kick/Extreme Heat impacts, throws, swing variants, grabs, finisher impact, guard break, heat gain/full, Heat Mode on/off and charge cues. Charge-end is also available for future charged-move work. Normal attacks select their original Yakuza impact variants through the framework's own weapon; vanilla BareHands and other weapons are untouched. Swing sounds are scheduled near contact and cancelled with interrupted attacks. Finisher accents occur once per swing rather than once per multihit victim. Heat gain sounds play only when a completed stock increases. The original Heat Mode on sequence has two layers, with its second layer delayed 300 ms. Cancellation stops pending activation layers and voices. Extra cues retain the existing sound toggle.

The old grab follower requested deferred movement, which could leave the zombie visibly behind the player. The follower now collision-checks the full displacement and synchronizes the zombie's current, next and previous/render positions plus its moving square. It follows the player's rendered animation facing while turning and is corrected after the zombie's native update. Deferred zombie root movement is disabled only while held and restored on release. The extra update hook exists only during an active grab. Throw alignment still explicitly snaps both current headings before the throw.

Extreme Heat activation now records its entry position, disables deferred/root movement, clears movement/running momentum and holds that position through the clip. Late native movement is corrected during the action, player update and a temporary tick hook. The original deferred-movement state is restored on completion or cancellation. Existing movement/protection cleanup and native-clip watchdogs remain in place.

All four guard phases now affect only Spine1 and its upper-body descendants/hand props. Guard no longer blocks player movement or ignores movement input, so normal Zomboid leg locomotion continues while holding RMB + X and moving. Frontal protection, stamina costs and guard-break recovery remain unchanged.

Offline verification includes combat lifecycle regressions, immediate follow/render-position synchronization, rendered-facing attachment, movement/momentum cancellation, state restoration, guard movement, native cue parsing and source/output audio integrity. Every graphical texture and every existing animation clip remains unchanged; only the four guard animation masks change. PZ is not launched as part of installation.
