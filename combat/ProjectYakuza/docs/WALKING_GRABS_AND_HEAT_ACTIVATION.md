# Walking grabs and animated Extreme Heat — 0.19.0

After the paired grab entry finishes, use normal movement keys while aiming to walk with the zombie. Kiryu's `sy0_gsp_wlk_lp` and the matching `sy1` original clips provide the grabbing motion. The player's hold/walk nodes mask only Spine1 and its upper-body descendants, leaving native Zomboid locomotion in control of the legs. Five victim clips combine original grabbing upper-body motion with native Zomboid idle/forward/backward/left/right leg motion. Original upper-body and native lower-body cycle durations are preserved; the upper-body loop is not sped up to fit a shorter leg cycle.

The zombie follows at the fixed grip offset through swept collision checks. Obstructed movement releases the pair rather than crossing a wall. MMB realigns the zombie to the player at the exact paired root offset and snaps both current animation headings before entering the full-body throw. Both characters then remain locked through the throw. The existing hold timeout, stamina cost and damage/unequip interruption rules remain in force.

Normal hit visuals now use five small world-space particles from the existing original centred `HitGlint.png`: one pulsing contact glint and four outward-moving, shrinking sparks. The corner-origin fan texture is no longer used. Scaling compensates the native bottom anchor each frame, keeping the contact point fixed. Normal effects last 240 ms; stronger effects last 280 ms. Throw and Tiger Drop impacts also receive a short burst, including on kills. The existing effect toggle and bounded pool remain available; cleanup and rendering hooks are removed while idle.

N while aiming selects one of Kiryu's four original `P_KRU_BTL_SUD_bct_std_[A-D]_st.gmt` activation clips, avoiding an immediate repeat. Their original durations are 1.5, 1.833, 2.0 and 2.267 seconds. Movement is locked while the activation plays. Temporary native ghost/invincible flags prevent zombie targeting and queued bite damage; nearby attacks targeting the player are interrupted. Completion, cancellation, timeouts, unequip, save and world exit restore the previous flags and movement state. Recovery metadata also restores those flags after an interrupted session. God mode and health restoration are not used.

Heat is preserved during the activation and only committed to Extreme Heat after the native clip-end marker. Missing or interrupted animations cancel without consuming the stored heat. Fuel duration, six stocks, damage balance, existing combo animations, aura and HUD graphics are unchanged.

Combat feedback reuses existing PZ audio events: normal punches keep native BareHandsHit; confirmed finishers add one low impact layer per swing, with rustle/impact cues for grabs, throws, Tiger Drop and activation. Extra sounds can be toggled in the combat-style menu. Brief action messages also report grab, throw, guard break, Tiger Drop and activation. No new audio recordings or visual textures were generated.

Offline validation: 1,743 Kahlua regression checks; 22 native guard/grab phase selections; four native activation selections; 240 native world-projection cases across facings, tile scales, character offsets and animation ages; native importer acceptance of all 11 added clips; 18,720 native lower-body rotation/position comparisons. Every PNG/DDS is byte-identical to the prior release. PZ was not launched, as requested; visual fit and sound balance still need an in-game check.
